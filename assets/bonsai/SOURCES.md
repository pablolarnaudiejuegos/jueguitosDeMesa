# Original artwork

Source: DV Games, Bonsai English rulebook, pages 2 and 12.
https://www.dvgiochi.com/giochi/bonsai-eng/download/Bonsai_ENG-Rules_WEB.pdf

Four tile faces and illustration excerpts for each card family were rendered from the PDF. Printed example effect symbols were excluded; the application supplies each card actual effect separately. Parchment uses its paper texture. These are not scans of all 47 individual cards. Original artwork belongs to its respective rights holders; no redistribution license is asserted.
